Four tigers. Twenty goats. One board.

Bagh Chal (also written Baghchal or Bagh-Chal) is the traditional "move the tiger" game of Nepal. The tigers hunt: capture five goats and they win. The goats defend: work together, trap every tiger and they win. Easy to learn in a minute, deep enough to play for years.

New to Bagh Chal? The rules, in short:
• Tigers start in the four corners. Goats come onto the board one at a time, then move.
• Pieces move along the lines to the next point. A tiger captures by jumping over a goat to the empty point behind it.
• Tigers win by capturing five goats. Goats win when no tiger can move.
