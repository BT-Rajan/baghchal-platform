# Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data ClawPlay collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. We wrote it from what our software actually stores. If anything here is unclear, write to us at [[contact_email]].

## Who we are

ClawPlay is run by [[operator_name]], [[operator_address]] ("we", "us"). We decide how and why your data is used: under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we are the *Data Fiduciary*, and under the EU and UK GDPR the *controller*.

Grievance Officer: [[grievance_officer]], [[grievance_email]].

## What we collect, and why

**Your account**

- Username, email address, and your password, which we store only as a strong one-way hash (Argon2id). We use these to create your account, sign you in, and send emails you need (confirming your address, resetting your password, notices about your account).
- Which versions of these terms and this policy you accepted, and when.
- Your session: a random sign-in token, which we store only as a hash, with when it was created and last used.

**Playing**

- Your games: every move, how long you took over it, the clocks, results and ratings. Games are what the service is; your opponents see them too, and finished games appear in your public game history.
- Challenges (by name or by link), quick-pairing requests, tournaments, puzzles you tried, your friends and friend requests, players you blocked, and your board, piece, sound and quick-message choices.
- Notifications we show you in the app (someone challenged you, asked to be friends, a tournament started).
- Achievements are worked out from your games and puzzles whenever they are shown; nothing extra is stored.
- Quick messages in games (such as "Good luck") are passed to your opponent and not stored.
- Chat messages in games: players with an account (not guests) can write to each other during a game and for a few minutes after it, unless either of them turned messages off. We keep what you write for 30 days, so that if someone reports a message a moderator can read it, and we hide words on our blocked list.
- Whether you are online right now (kept only in short-lived memory, not stored).

**Fair play and safety**

- Reports you make or that are made about you, and what moderators did about them.
- Automatic fair-play figures: after each rated game our engine reviews the moves and keeps a summary for each player (how often you played its move, how much you gave away, how long you took). These are used only to decide whether a moderator should look at your games. They are never used to penalise you automatically.

**Payments**

- If you buy ClawPlay Plus or Pro: the plan, price, and the order and payment numbers from Razorpay. Your card, UPI or bank details go straight to Razorpay; we never see or store them.

**Technical**

- To limit abuse (such as password guessing), we count requests by IP address for up to 24 hours. We do not store IP addresses in our database or logs.
- Error reports (when something breaks) contain no personal data.
- One cookie, which keeps you signed in on the web. We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies, and we do not sell or rent your data, ever.

## Why we may use it (legal grounds)

- **To run the service you signed up for** (your account, games, ratings, payments): this is the purpose you consented to when you created your account, and under the GDPR it is necessary to perform our contract with you.
- **To keep play fair and safe** (rate limits, reports, moderation, fair-play checks): for the same purpose, and under the GDPR our legitimate interest in a fair game for everyone.
- **To meet legal duties** (keeping payment records, answering lawful requests).

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account (see *Your rights*). Withdrawing does not affect what was done before.

## Who else sees your data

- **Other players** see your username, ratings, online status, achievements, your games and your game history. Your friends see that you are friends. Nobody sees your email address.
- **Your opponent** sees the chat messages you write to them in a game.
- **Moderators** see reports, moderation records and fair-play figures, and the chat of a game when they check a report about it, to do their job.
- **Service providers** who process data for us, only as we instruct:
  - Razorpay (payments, India);
  - our email provider, to deliver emails: [[email_provider]];
  - our hosting provider, where our servers and backups run: [[hosting_provider]];
- **Authorities**, when the law requires us to.

We never sell personal data, and we share none for advertising.

## Where your data is stored

Our servers are in [[hosting_location]]. Our email and hosting providers may process data outside India or your country. Where the GDPR applies, such transfers rely on adequacy decisions or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account and games**: while your account exists.
- **When you delete your account**: your email address, password, sessions, links, blocks and chat messages are deleted at once. Your username is replaced with an anonymous one; games you played stay in your opponents' history under that anonymous name, without anything that identifies you.
- **Sign-ups never confirmed**: deleted after 7 days.
- **Guest accounts** (playing without signing up): deleted like an account when you sign out, or once their session expires, 30 days after you started playing.
- **Sessions**: deleted when they expire, 30 days after you sign in.
- **Email links** (confirmations, password resets): deleted 7 days after they expire or are used.
- **Notifications**: your newest 100.
- **Chat messages**: 30 days, then deleted (and at once if you delete your account).
- **Fair-play figures**: 1 year.
- **Reports and moderation records**: 3 years after they were dealt with.
- **Payment records**: 8 years, as Indian accounting and tax law requires.
- **Backups**: 14 days.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

- **see your data**: download everything about your account from *Me → Settings → Download my data*;
- **correct it**: change your email address and password in the app; for anything else, write to us;
- **delete it**: delete your account from *Me → Settings → Delete account*, or ask us;
- **nominate someone** to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to (India): write to us;
- **complain to us**: write to our Grievance Officer; we acknowledge within 24 hours and answer within 15 days.

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you also have the rights to restrict or object to how we use your data and to data portability (the download above), and you may complain to your data protection authority. In India, if we do not resolve your grievance, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Children

You must be at least 13 to use ClawPlay, and if you are under 18 you need your parent's or guardian's permission (see our terms). You confirm this when you press Accept; we do not ask for your age or keep any record of it. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete their child's account at any time by writing to us.

We do not track, profile or target advertising at anyone, children included.

If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

## Security

Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes; everything travels encrypted (HTTPS); and access to our servers and backups is limited to the people who run them. If a breach ever puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Changes

When we change this policy, we change the date below and ask you to accept the new version the next time you open ClawPlay. We will not use data already collected in a new way without your consent.
